How to read this

What the number includes, and what it does not.

Duty is assessed on the CIF value — goods plus freight plus insurance — and the destination tax is then applied to the duty-paid amount. That stacking is why a modest duty rate can still produce a large arrival bill: the tax is charged on a figure that already contains duty, freight and insurance.

A sourcing or agent fee is not part of the customs value. It is your own cost of buying, not part of what the goods are worth, so it is added to the total but excluded from the duty base. This calculator models that correctly, which many quick estimates do not.

Form E matters more than anything else on this page. Under the ASEAN–China Free Trade Area, a valid certificate of origin can reduce or eliminate duty on Chinese-origin furniture. Whether it applies depends on the HS code and on the certificate being issued and presented correctly, which is why the duty field here defaults to zero rather than guessing.

Tax rates are examples. They are pre-filled to show the order of magnitude and are editable for a reason: rates change, and the figure that applies to you depends on your product classification and your registration status. Confirm both rates with your customs broker before you put this number in front of a client.

Not included: demurrage or detention if a container overstays its free time, storage at either end, currency movement between quotation and payment, and the cost of resolving a quality failure. All of them are real, and all are better budgeted as contingency than ignored.

Common questions

Questions the landed number raises.

FAQ

What is landed cost, and why not just use the FOB quote?

FOB is the factory price at the port of loading, so the margin calculation is already missing freight, insurance, duty, destination tax and inland delivery. Landed cost is FOB plus exactly those items - the figure your business actually pays.

FAQ

Is the freight input the same for every sailing?

No. Ocean freight is the input worth getting from a forwarder rather than estimating, because the same container can quote very differently by sailing, season and destination. The calculator uses your total ocean freight figure rather than guessing a per-container rate.

FAQ

Does the duty field default to zero because I get duty free?

Duty defaults to 0 because many furniture lines enter ASEAN duty-free under the ASEAN-China Free Trade Area with a valid Form E certificate of origin - not because duty is never owed. Whether it applies depends on the HS code and whether the certificate is issued and presented correctly.

FAQ

Where does the agent fee appear in the number?

Agent fees are a buying cost, not part of the customs value. They are added to the total but excluded from the duty base, which is why the calculator shows them separately.

FAQ

Does the result include demurrage or storage?

No. Demurrage, detention, currency movement and the cost of resolving a quality failure are excluded so the number stays clean. They are real, and better budgeted as contingency than silently added into landed cost.

FAQ

Are the duty and tax rates I am shown the right ones for me?

Duty depends on the HS code and on whether you hold the correct certificate of origin; destination tax is GST or VAT at your port of entry. Both fields are pre-filled only to show order of magnitude and are editable - confirm the figures that apply to you with your broker.
