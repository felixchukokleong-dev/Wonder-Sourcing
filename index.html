How it works

One team, from your brief to your warehouse door.

Sourcing furniture from China usually means juggling five vendors and a translator. We run the whole chain as one line item — you deal with one team, one invoice, one point of contact.

Full service breakdown →

STEP01

Brief

Send us your spec sheet, target price, order volume and delivery window — for a single hotel project or a recurring retail order. We reply with feasibility and a shortlist plan within 48 hours.

STEP02

Source

We shortlist three to five factories from our vetted network across Shunde and Lecong, negotiate FOB pricing directly, and send samples or factory video walkthroughs before you commit.

STEP03

Inspect

Our own QC staff — not the factory's — check production at three stages: pre-production materials, mid-line assembly, and pre-shipment packing. You get a photo report before anything ships.

STEP04

Consolidate

Orders from multiple factories are palletised and combined into a single container at our Foshan warehouse, cutting freight cost and the number of shipments landing at your port.

STEP05

Ship

We handle export customs declaration, book ocean freight to your nearest port, and prepare the commercial invoice, packing list and bill of lading your customs broker will need.

STEP06

Deliver

We track the container to arrival and stay reachable until it clears customs and lands at your warehouse — with full documentation and QC reports on file.

Why source here

Foshan is where the furniture already is.

Shunde and Lecong, both districts of Foshan, hold the largest concentration of furniture factories and wholesale markets in the world — Lecong Furniture City alone spans thousands of showrooms under one roof. Buyers who fly in expecting one factory usually find they need three: a frame maker, an upholsterer, a finisher. We already know which three.

Being based here means we visit production lines on foot, not by video call, and can react to a delay the same afternoon it happens.

Quality control

We inspect on your behalf, not the factory's.

  • Independent staff. Our QC inspectors are salaried by us, not commissioned by the factories they're checking.
  • Three inspection points. Materials on arrival, mid-production assembly, and a final pre-shipment check against your approved sample.
  • Photo & video reports. Delivered before loading, so you can approve or hold a shipment from anywhere.
  • Claims support. If something ships faulty, we hold the factory to the fix — it's our relationship on the line, every order.
