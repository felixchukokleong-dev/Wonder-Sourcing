What the fee covers

Included — and passed through at cost.

Everything we do sits in the fee; everything we buy on your behalf is passed through at cost. If it is not on this list, it is not on your invoice.

FEEIncluded

Covered by our fee

  • Factory shortlisting and vetting: 3–5 audited factories per category, business licence, export history and capacity verified on site
  • FOB negotiation: negotiated directly — no markup hidden inside the unit cost
  • Sample coordination: physical samples or factory video walkthroughs, revisions logged against your brief
  • Three-stage inspection: incoming materials, mid-line assembly and pre-shipment packing, by our salaried inspectors
  • Consolidation and loading: multi-factory orders palletised into one container, photographed before sealing
  • Export paperwork: commercial invoice, packing list and bill of lading arranged with the forwarder
COSTPassed through

  • Goods: the factory FOB price, shown to you as quoted
  • Ocean freight and insurance: forwarder rates, passed through line for line

  • Samples and courier: physical sample and shipping charges

Pricing questions

Common questions about our fees.

What drives the size of your quote?

Category count, number of factories, specification complexity and revision rounds — a one-product order from one workshop costs less to run than casegoods, upholstery and outdoor lines across four factories with consolidation. Same drivers as the industry-wide fees guide describes.

Is there a minimum order or minimum fee?

No minimum for factory audits or QC-only inspection. For full sourcing, most first orders fill a quarter to a full 40ft container — we will tell you honestly if your volume is too small to consolidate efficiently.

How do retainer terms work?

A percentage of FOB value agreed upfront, reviewed against your actual order cadence. You get a dedicated account contact and priority QC scheduling across every category on the programme.

What does QC-only inspection cost relative to full service?

A fraction — you keep the sourcing and negotiation, and pay only for independent audits and pre-shipment inspection. It suits buyers with an existing factory relationship who want a second pair of eyes.

What is never inside your fee?

The goods, freight and insurance, duty and tax at your port, and any design or compliance engineering — and we will never take commission from a factory. Anything you pay us is on the quote, in writing, before work starts.
