Good to know

Notes for buyers shipping to Thailand.

  • Laem Chabang is Thailand's main deep-sea port and typically offers more frequent sailings than Bangkok Port itself.
  • Resort projects on Phuket and Koh Samui usually route through Laem Chabang before onward domestic transport.
Customs and duty

Clearance into Laem Chabang.

Duty on furniture into Thailand follows the HS code declared for each item, with VAT applied on top — the calculator pre-fills 7% as an example only. Thailand is part of the ASEAN–China free trade area, so Chinese-origin goods can qualify for preferential rates with a valid Form E certificate of origin. Where a shipment mixes categories, each line needs its own correct classification.

Resort projects add a wrinkle: goods often land at Laem Chabang and then move onward by road and sea to Phuket or Koh Samui. That final leg runs on its own schedule, separate from the ocean freight, and it is the one most often left out of the plan. We build the packing around the onward transfer, so cartons can be handled more than once without failing.

Outdoor specification

What resort furniture has to survive here.

Outdoor and poolside furniture bound for Thailand faces salt air, monsoon rain and near-constant humidity. Three choices decide whether it lasts: the finish system on any metal frame, the foam and fabric used in cushions, and the fixings — because a screw that rusts will stain the material around it long before it fails outright.

None of those are visible in a photograph, which is why the specification has to be written down and the sample approved physically rather than from images. We ask for the substrate, the finish schedule and the cushion specification in writing on outdoor orders, and we record what the approved sample represents so pre-shipment inspection has something concrete to measure against.

Watch out for

Three things that catch Thailand buyers.

  • High-season opening dates. A resort aiming to open for peak season has no slack. Production, inspection and the island transfer all have to finish before that date, and each can slip independently of the others.
  • Double handling on the onward leg. Cargo that tranships at Laem Chabang and again at the island gets handled more times than a single-container delivery. Carton strength matters more here than the per-unit saving on a thinner box.
  • Specifying outdoor furniture indoors. Choosing by appearance in a showroom, without confirming the finish system and cushion materials, is how weather-rated furniture ends up replaced within two seasons.

What is the main category of furniture exported to Thailand?

Is there import duty on furniture going into Thailand?

Duty follows the HS code declared for each item, with VAT applied on top. Thailand is part of the ASEAN-China free trade area, so Chinese-origin goods can qualify for preferential rates when the certificate of origin is provided.

How should outdoor furniture be specified for Thailand?

Outdoor and poolside furniture faces salt air, monsoon rain and near-constant humidity. None of that shows in a photograph, so the finish system and cushion materials need to be written into the specification rather than chosen from a showroom sample.

What usually goes wrong on Thailand shipments?

Three things catch Thailand buyers: high-season opening dates that leave no slack for a rework loop, double handling where cargo moves onward from Laem Chabang to an island, and specifying outdoor furniture from an indoor showroom sample.
