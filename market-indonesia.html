Customs and duty

Clearance into Tanjung Priok.

Duty follows the HS code declared per item, with VAT (PPN) applied on top — the calculator pre-fills 11% as an example only. Indonesia is inside the ASEAN–China free trade area, so Chinese-origin goods can qualify for preferential treatment with a valid Form E certificate of origin, agreeing with the invoice and packing list line for line.

Tanjung Priok is the slowest of the ports we route to, and clearance at Jakarta is typically the longest leg of the whole journey. That is not a reason to avoid the market; it is a reason to start earlier, and to settle your broker and licensing arrangements before the first container rather than during it.

Sourcing strategy

Importing what Indonesia does not already make.

Indonesia has a substantial furniture industry of its own and exports teak and rattan work at scale, so competing head-on with local production is usually the wrong move. The orders we handle here tend to be categories where Chinese manufacturing is genuinely differentiated: hotel-grade casegoods built to a fixed specification, metal-framed commercial seating, and items where consistency across hundreds of identical units matters more than the character of an individual piece.

Buyers who get the most from this market identify the specific gap first — a range or a tolerance their local supply cannot hold across volume — and import that, rather than importing an entire furniture package.

Watch out for

Three things that catch Indonesia buyers.

  • The longest transit in this list. Seven to ten days by sea before clearance even begins. A schedule built on the transit times of Thailand or Vietnam will be late here.
  • Broker experience with furniture codes. Upholstered goods, mixed-material pieces and flat-pack kits classify differently and attract different treatment. Confirm your broker has handled your categories before the first shipment, not after.
  • Assuming one clearance fits every entry point. Arrangements that run smoothly through Jakarta may not transfer to another port without review.
